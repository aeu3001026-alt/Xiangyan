New Zealand culture is a dynamic synthesis of indigenous Māori heritage, British colonial roots, and vibrant Pacific and Asian influences. Often described as "Kiwis," New Zealanders are known for their egalitarianism, deep connection to the outdoors, and laid-back friendliness.

New Zealand society is modern, egalitarian, and increasingly multicultural, defined by a strong connection to the natural environment, a laid-back lifestyle, and a deep appreciation for a healthy work-life balance.
